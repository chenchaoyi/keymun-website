Welcome Letter

Dear students,

Welcome to KEYMUN!

Through simulation of real UN councils and heated discussions of pressing global issues, KEYMUN is dedicated to fostering global awareness and intercultural dialogue, while also highlighting the unique perspectives of China and the wider international community.

In this inaugural edition of KEYMUN, we are excited to offer three diverse committees, including one conducted fully in Chinese, embodying Keystone's bilingual identity and commitment to a strong Chinese thread.

This year's theme, "Rebuilding Trust in a Fragmented World," not only reflects the shared emphasis of all three committees' topics on the search for collective order but also carries our vision for a future built on the fundamental human bonds and connections between diverse groups. Thus, we wish for every delegate to develop international-mindedness and lasting friendships through this two-and-a-half-day meaningful experience at Keystone.

With excitement,

KEYMUN Secretary General
