About KEYMUN

Keystone Model United Nations

Our Mission

Keystone Model United Nations (KEYMUN) is dedicated to fostering global awareness and intercultural dialogue through the simulation of real UN councils and heated discussions of pressing global issues. We highlight the unique perspectives of China and the wider international community, embodying Keystone Academy's commitment to bilingual education and a strong Chinese thread.

In this inaugural edition of KEYMUN, we offer three diverse committees — including one conducted fully in Chinese — reflecting our belief that meaningful diplomacy transcends linguistic boundaries.

Our 2026 theme, "Rebuilding Trust in a Fragmented World," reflects the shared emphasis of all three committees' topics on the search for collective order and carries our vision for a future built on the fundamental human bonds and connections between diverse groups.
